ChainPlanner: Long-Horizon Planning for World Action Models

2026 · on top of the frozen ten-task Phoenix-MWA · real dual-arm robot

details/code

Designed a 2B high-level planner on top of the frozen ten-task Phoenix-MWA: one instruction becomes a skill plan, and a language memory tracks the current step. Trained only on random chains built from single-task episodes, with no long-horizon data.

Chain replay 260 / 300 with no wrong switch; real robot 22 / 26

π0.6 RECAP Real-Robot Self-Improvement (AgileX PiperX Arm)

2026 · FMC3-Robotics · owned end to end

details

Reproduced π0.6 RECAP on an AgileX PiperX arm: 200 demonstrations for a cold start, offline RL with advantage conditioning, then several rounds of real-robot self-improvement from autonomous rollouts and expert corrections.

Benchmark throughput ≈2×, failures roughly halved

details

Built a dual-arm platform with an AgiBot dexterous hand from scratch on LeRobot, fused a tactile encoder into π0.5, and paired it with RTC async inference.

PiPER Arm Operating an Android Tablet with a GUI Agent

2026 · camera-only (no ADB) · real single-arm robot

details/code

Spoken or typed instructions (SenseVoiceSmall) drive a PiPER arm holding a 3D-printed compliant stylus to operate an Android tablet using cameras alone: GUI-Owl-1.5 plans taps and swipes on rectified wrist-camera views, backed by Pinocchio IK, hand-eye calibration, ArUco tracking, and leave-one-out tap-error correction.
