ChainPlanner: Long-Horizon Planning for World Action Models

Trained a 2B high-level planner (RynnBrain1.1-2B + LoRA) on synthetic chains of existing single-task episodes—no long-horizon data—to orchestrate a frozen ten-task Phoenix-MWA using a plan-once, track-steps language memory.

RoboSynChallenge 2026 · 1st Place, Simulation Track · Team Lead

Used a digital twin to pinpoint wrist-camera pose shift as the sim-to-real failure cause, then post-trained Phoenix-MWA on 32×A100 using real data only. Found that failed pre-takeover lead-ins and pauses over 0.5 s taught the policy to stall, and fixed it with takeover-segment trimming, pause collapsing, and RTC soft-mask action-chunk inpainting.

π0.6 RECAP Real-Robot Self-Improvement (AgileX PiperX Arm)

Reproduced π0.6 RECAP on an AgileX PiperX arm (phone plug-in benchmark): 200 demonstrations for a cold start, offline RL with advantage conditioning, then multi-round real-robot self-improvement from autonomous rollouts and expert corrections paired with action chunking and async inference.

Robotic Arm + AgiBot Dexterous Hand Dual-Arm Collaboration

Built a dual-arm platform with an AgiBot dexterous hand from scratch on LeRobot; adding a tactile encoder to π0.5 together with RTC async inference raised dual-arm fine-manipulation system success from 30% to 70%.

PiPER Arm Operating an Android Tablet with a GUI Agent

Voice or text instructions (SenseVoiceSmall) drive a PiPER arm holding a 3D-printed compliant stylus to operate an Android tablet using cameras alone: rectified wrist-camera views feed vLLM-served GUI-Owl-1.5 (0.75–1.5 s/step), backed by Pinocchio IK, hand-eye calibration (0.11 px / 0.02°), ArUco tracking, and leave-one-out tap-error correction.
